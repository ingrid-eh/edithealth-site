{EditHealth.ai} templates for the first investigator's brochure

A bespoke Microsoft Word template for the first investigator's brochure, written before a first-in-human (FIH) clinical trial, for freelance medical writers and small biotech teams.

Settled information

Final nonclinical results such as no-adverse-effect levels, exposure and pharmacokinetics, and manufacturing facts such as strength and storage conditions, arrive prefilled from the Evidence Envelope with their sources. Each toxicology finding is tagged with its SEND dataset and domain, so the text matches the datasets FDA receives.

Judged information

Whether an animal finding matters for people, the starting dose and the exposure margin behind it, and which risks investigators should watch for stay with your toxicologist, clinical pharmacologist and medical lead.

Curating

You write the document in Microsoft Word, in your own style, with a sidebar such as Claude for Word or Copilot for Word. No one drafts it for you to approve.

Each decision appears as a question addressed to its named owner. Once answered, the decision is reused word for word in every linked document: the protocol, the investigator's brochure and the introductory statement. Guidance notes and questions are styled to delete at publishing. Claude or Copilot in the sidebar handles transcription and checks, never the decisions.

Support for the decisions that matter

Toxicologists, biostatisticians and safety scientists are available on demand to review the decisions that matter in your program: species relevance, the starting-dose basis, adverse events of special interest and the stopping rules.
